## Captured Decisions

- Preserve the current mobile-first Expo/React Native product posture.
- Preserve the existing bottom tab pattern for v1 crop app continuity, but rename and reorganize surfaces around farm work rather than implementation artifacts.
- Preserve the current top farm header, local/offline indicator, floating add action, sheet modals, card/list composition, calendar month view, backfill chips, expandable record rows, photo linking, PDF/CSV export, and simple plain-English reporting labels.
- Redesign generic record entry into structured, record-type-specific sheets for fertilizer, chemical, watering, labour, expense, harvest, sale, and payment.
- Treat the current `Fields` screen as the seed for a future `Production` surface that supports farms, enterprises, production units, and cycles without hard-coding chilli.
- Keep crop-specific guidance separate from general recordkeeping. ABE Chilli guidance may appear as a crop guidance layer, not as the core product model.
- Make offline state visible and reassuring in v1. For future sync, distinguish "saved on this phone", "waiting to sync", "synced", "needs review", and "conflict".
- Keep forms short by default. Reveal extra fields only when they materially improve traceability, safety, finance, or reporting.
- Use plain-English labels such as "Total spent", "Total earned", "Net profit", "Cost of fertilizer", "Sales income", and "Money still owed".
- Treat current placeholder rows in `More` as future real surfaces for Inventory, Suppliers, Photo records, and Offline sync.

## Screen Classification
